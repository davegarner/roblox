One entry per session. Five minutes at the end of each session is enough.
In Claude Code just say: "add a journal entry for today" and it will draft one for you to correct.

## How to fill an entry

- **Built**: what exists now that did not exist before (a feature, a fix, a script).
- **Learned**: one or two concepts *in your own words*. If you cannot explain it, you have not learned it yet. Ask Claude to explain it again a different way.
- **Stuck / questions**: anything that confused you. Start the next session with these.
- **Next**: the single next thing to do. Keep it small.
- **Commit**: the message or hash from `git log --oneline -1`.
